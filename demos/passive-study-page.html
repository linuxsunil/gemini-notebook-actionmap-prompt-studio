Recognising High-Risk Phishing Indicators

Employees must treat any email, Slack message, or file-sharing prompt as suspicious if it exhibits one or more of the four high-risk indicators defined in SOP-SEC-04.

Artificial Urgency

Indicator 01

Demands for immediate action engineered to rush your judgement and prevent verification.

SOP Examples: “within 30 minutes”, “account will be locked today”, or an “urgent CEO request”.

Domain Mismatches

Indicator 02

Lookalike web addresses or external senders masquerading as internal executives or trusted software providers.

SOP Examples: c0mpany-hr.com, microsoft-support-login.net, or executive impersonation.

Unusual Attachments or Links

Indicator 03

Unexpected file formats capable of executing scripts or disguised links routing to external sign-in pages.

SOP Examples: Unexpected .zip, .html, macro-enabled Office files, or shortened URLs to external login portals.

PII or Credential Requests

Indicator 04

Any attempt to obtain sensitive authentication secrets or personal/corporate records through informal channels.

SOP Examples: Passwords, MFA codes, employee SSNs, payroll spreadsheets, or customer lists via email, Slack, or unapproved file-sharing tools.

Mandatory Response & Containment Rules

SOP-SEC-04 separates response procedures into immediate handling of suspicious messages and rapid containment if an interaction has already occurred.

Out-of-Band Verification

Mandatory Rule

Always verify urgent financial, access, or data requests through an independent, trusted channel.

  • Call the sender using their known internal directory number.
  • Or message them in a separate, verified Slack thread.
  • Never use telephone numbers or links included inside the suspicious message.

15-Minute Reporting Protocol

Mandatory Rule

Submit suspicious messages to Information Security within 15 minutes using one of two approved paths:

  • Click the Report Phish button directly in your email client.
  • Or capture a screenshot (without opening any attachments) and raise a ticket with security@company.com.

If You Have Already Clicked or Shared Data

Never hide it. There is no disciplinary penalty for reporting an accidental click immediately; concealing a breach violates company policy.

  • Disconnect from the corporate VPN and Wi-Fi at once if a file was downloaded.
  • Contact the InfoSec Hotline (ext. 4400) immediately so the team can revoke session tokens and force a password and MFA reset within 10 minutes.
