SOP-SEC-04 Quick-Reference

Phishing & Data Privacy Response · Jan 2026

1. Business Objective

Reduce successful credential-harvesting and unauthorised data-exfiltration incidents to zero by verifying unusual requests out-of-band and reporting suspicious messages within 15 minutes.

2. High-Risk Indicators

  • Artificial Urgency: Demands for immediate action (“within 30 minutes”, “account will be locked today”, “urgent CEO request”).
  • Domain Mismatches: Lookalike domains (e.g., c0mpany-hr.com, microsoft-support-login.net) or external senders impersonating internal executives.
  • Unusual Attachments or Links: Unexpected .zip, .html, or macro-enabled Office files, or shortened URLs leading to external login portals.
  • PII or Credential Requests: Requests to share passwords, MFA codes, employee SSNs, payroll spreadsheets, or customer lists over email, Slack, or unapproved file-sharing tools.

3. Mandatory Response Actions

DO NOT:
  • Click links, download attachments, or reply to the sender — even to tell them you know it is a scam.
  • Click “Unsubscribe” on suspicious external emails; this confirms your address is active.
  • Forward the suspicious email to colleagues or your manager to ask “Is this real?” Forwarding spreads the payload.
DO:
  • Verify urgent financial, access, or data requests out-of-band (call the person on their known internal directory number or message them in a separate, verified Slack thread — never using contact info inside the suspicious message).
  • Report the message within 15 minutes using the Report Phish button in your email client, or by taking a screenshot (without opening attachments) and submitting a ticket to security@company.com.

4. If You Already Clicked or Shared Data

  • Never hide it. There is no disciplinary penalty for reporting an accidental click immediately; hiding a breach violates company policy.
  • Disconnect from the corporate VPN/Wi-Fi if a file was downloaded.
  • Contact the InfoSec Hotline (ext. 4400) immediately so the team can revoke session tokens and force a password/MFA reset within 10 minutes.
